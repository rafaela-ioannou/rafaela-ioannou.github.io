The reading group on Geometric Group Theory was ran as part of the EPSRC Centre for Doctoral Training in Algebra, Geometry and Quantum Fields (AGQ), during semester 2 of the 2025-2026 academic year. It was aimed for students following [this course on Geometric Group Theory](https://smstc.ac.uk/modules/introduction-to-geometric-group-theory), with each student presenting a topic of interest going beyond the course. Examples of topics include boundaries of groups and generalisations of hyperbolicity.
