Random groups was ran as a reading group through the EPSRC Centre for Doctoral Training in Algebra, Geometry and Quantum Fields (AGQ), organised by Talia Shlomovich and myself. It ran during semester 2 of the 2025-2026 academic year. Participants followed resourses on Random Groups, including topics such as probability distributions, Gromov's 1/2 density theorem and Stalling's graphs.
